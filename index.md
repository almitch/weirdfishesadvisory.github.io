<section class="problem-grid-section">
  <h2>Four problems I solve</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">Every client I work with faces at least one of these. Most face several. The order below reflects a natural journey — but you can enter at any point.</p>

  <div class="problem-grid">
    <div class="problem-card">
      <span class="problem-number">01</span>
      <h3>"We don't have the right systems to work with our data."</h3>
      <p>You collect data but lack the infrastructure — the pipelines, platforms, or internal processes — to make it usable. I audit what you have, design what you need (scaled to your team's capacity and budget), and build or oversee the technical delivery. The system I leave behind is one you can actually maintain.</p>
      <div class="proof-point">
        <p>Rebuilt Ocean Wise's whale alert system from multiple disconnected projects into a single platform with integrated data pipelines. Introduced GitHub-based workflows and R Shiny dashboarding at DEFRA, transforming how analytical teams stored and quality-assured their work.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="problem-card">
      <span class="problem-number">02</span>
      <h3>"We have data but it's not driving our decisions."</h3>
      <p>The infrastructure exists but the data isn't reaching decision-makers or shaping strategy. I build the decision-support tools — dashboards, metrics frameworks, reporting structures — that put the right information in front of the right people.</p>
      <div class="proof-point">
        <p>Built the monitoring tool for DEFRA's £640M England Tree Planting Programme — cited as a "model example of reporting" by the Chief Analyst. Delivered real-time COVID-19 and EU Exit situation reports enabling ministers and No.10 to respond to emerging crises.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="problem-card">
      <span class="problem-number">03</span>
      <h3>"We can't demonstrate our impact."</h3>
      <p>You're doing valuable work but can't prove it — to funders, to government, or to your own board. I design impact frameworks, build clear reporting and visualisation, and help you tell the story of your work in a way that is compelling and grounded in evidence.</p>
      <div class="proof-point">
        <p>Developed the impact reporting framework for Future Fit Foundation, translating UN SDGs into measurable business benchmarks. Presented complex environmental evidence to the Secretary of State and senior ministers — translating analysis into actionable policy narratives.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="problem-card">
      <span class="problem-number">04</span>
      <h3>"Our programme should be bigger than it is."</h3>
      <p>You have a strong environmental programme but it's stuck — not reaching enough users, not engaging the right stakeholders, or remaining voluntary when it should be embedded in regulation. I handle the strategic layer: stakeholder mapping, partnership development, and the advocacy needed to scale.</p>
      <div class="proof-point">
        <p>Doubled the WRAS user base and increased data availability by 200% through six new partnerships, expanding coverage from British Columbia to continental North America. Currently leading integration of whale data into regulated maritime navigation — moving the programme from voluntary to operational policy.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
