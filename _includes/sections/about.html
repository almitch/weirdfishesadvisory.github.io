I've spent seven years at the intersection of environmental data, policy and strategy: in UK government at DEFRA, where I built the monitoring tool behind the £640M England Tree Planting Programme and briefed ministers during COVID-19 and EU Exit; at Ocean Wise, where I ran the Whale Report Alert System programme; and in field programmes on three continents.

I build the systems and frameworks that turn nature data into decisions, defensible impact and policy that scales. That means coding a dashboard in the morning and presenting strategy to a board in the afternoon. I'm a marine scientist by training (MRes, UCL), and I work in R, Python, SQL and GIS.
