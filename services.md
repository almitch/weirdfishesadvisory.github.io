    <h2>Service areas</h2>
    <span class="scroll-hint"><i class="fas fa-arrow-right"></i> drag to explore</span>

  <div class="services-scroll-track">

    <div class="service-scroll-card">
      <span class="card-number">01</span>
      <span class="card-label">Measurement Design</span>
      <h2>New or existing, how do we measure and use data?</h2>
      <p class="card-body">Programmes are often built without a process to capture whether it's working. We start from the impact you're trying to prove and work backwards: what would demonstrate it, what data generates that evidence, and how that data should be collected, structured, and stored. Working from user stories and the real capacity of your team, we produce the measurement framework and system design your developers can build against - so the right data is captured from day one rather than reconstructed later.</p>
      <div class="card-deliverables">
        <h3>What you receive</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Impact-to-data map - what you need to prove, and the data that proves it</li>
          <li>Monitoring &amp; evaluation framework - indicators per component, site, or deployment</li>
          <li>Data collection design - what's captured, by whom, and through what process</li>
          <li>System architecture &amp; requirements - user stories and a brief your developers can build from</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="service-scroll-card">
      <span class="card-number">02</span>
      <span class="card-label">Analysis &amp; Communication</span>
      <h2>You've got the data - but what does it mean, and who needs to hear it?</h2>
      <p class="card-body">Data competency and literacy can be difficult to build within teams, leading plenty of organisations to be sitting on data that could be doing more, or producing reports that don't land with decision makers. We help you interpret what your data is actually telling you, build the impact framework that separates what you can evidence from what you can't yet, and turn the result into something a funder, board, or government audience will understand and trust. This isn't just making a pretty graph - it's strategic communication to tell a compelling story using your impact.</p>
      <div class="card-deliverables">
        <h3>What you receive</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Impact framework - what you claim, what you can evidence, and how the gap closes</li>
          <li>Analysis &amp; interpretation - what your data shows, in plain terms</li>
          <li>Funder- and board-ready reporting - narrative and visuals built for a specific audience</li>
          <li>A product your team will understand how to use</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="service-scroll-card">
      <span class="card-number">03</span>
      <span class="card-label">Decision-Support Tools</span>
      <h2>Can the right people reach your data when a decision depends on it?</h2>
      <p class="card-body">Data that lives in spreadsheets or databases alone isn't driving decisions. We build the tools that put the right information, in the right format, in front of the right people, so decisions are driven by evidence - dashboards, reporting frameworks, and decision-support systems scaled to your team and budget. Where a build is straightforward we'll do it directly (Excel, Power BI, R, Python); where it's more complex we'll write the requirements brief so you can commission the right partner with confidence. Either way you're left with something your team can run without us.</p>
      <div class="card-deliverables">
        <h3>What you receive</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Decision-support tools - dashboards and reporting built for how your people actually work</li>
          <li>Technical requirements brief - a procurement-ready spec where an external build is needed</li>
          <li>Handover documentation - so your team can maintain what's been built</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="service-scroll-card">
      <span class="card-number">04</span>
      <span class="card-label">Strategy &amp; Scaling</span>
      <h2>Where should this programme be in five years - and how does it get there?</h2>
      <p class="card-body">Whether you're struggling to really make change, or starting a new project or programme, we can create and run that thinking with you. Through facilitated workshops we surface the assumptions your programme rests on and build a theory of change that makes its logic explicit and testable. From there we develop the strategy: the priorities, the growth pathway, and - where scaling is the goal - how you align with the frameworks that govern your field (IMO, IPBES, CBD, IWC) and build the evidence-based case to embed your work in policy. We're doing exactly this with WRAS, developing the strategy that's moving it from a voluntary app in British Columbia toward regulated maritime navigation across continental North America.</p>
      <div class="card-deliverables">
        <h3>What you receive</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Facilitated workshops - structured sessions that surface assumptions and build alignment across your team, board, or partners</li>
          <li>Theory of change - how your activities lead to outcomes and impact, made explicit and testable</li>
          <li>Stakeholder &amp; landscape analysis - who matters, where the leverage is, and which frameworks govern your field</li>
          <li>Programme strategy - priorities and a growth pathway, with a clear rationale behind each decision</li>
          <li>Advocacy &amp; scaling plan - the route from good idea to embedded policy, where scaling is the goal</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>
