### What it is

I design and build decision-support dashboards, data systems, and analytical tools that transform raw data into actionable insights for conservation teams. These aren't just visualizations—they're operational systems that integrate into your workflow.

### How it helps

~ **Faster decisions:** Leadership gets real-time access to critical data
~ **Better resource allocation:** Understand where your efforts have the most impact
~ **Reduced manual work:** Automate data collection and reporting processes
~ **Evidence-based strategy:** Ground decisions in data, not just intuition
~ **Stakeholder communication:** Visualize complex data for boards and funders

### What you get

~ Custom dashboards tailored to your KPIs and decision-making needs
~ Data pipeline development (integration with existing systems)
~ Interactive visualizations and reporting tools
~ Documentation and training for your team
~ Ongoing support and iteration as needs evolve

### Who it's for

~ Conservation program managers who need to track multiple initiatives
~ Leadership teams making resource allocation decisions
~ Organizations reporting to boards, donors, or government agencies
~ Teams drowning in spreadsheets who need a better system

### Example projects

**Government Decision Tool:** Built a real-time dashboard for UK DEFRA to track policy outcomes during COVID-19, integrating multiple data sources and providing scenario modeling for leadership.

**Marine Conservation Monitoring:** Developed an impact tracking system for an international NGO managing 20+ field programs, reducing reporting time by 70% while improving data quality.
