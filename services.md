      <p class="card-body">Environmental organisations often make impact claims they can't yet defend.       <span class="card-number">02</span>
      <span class="card-label">MEL Framework Design</span>
      <h2>Build the monitoring infrastructure your programme actually needs.</h2>
      <p class="card-tagline">Not bolted on after the fact.</p>
      <p class="card-body">Most organisations bolt monitoring onto programmes after the fact - then scramble at reporting time. We design MEL frameworks from the ground up, aligned to your theory of change, funder requirements, and the real capacity of your team. The result is a system that generates useful data for internal decision-making, not just compliance.</p>

        <h3>What you receive</h3>
        <ul>

          <li>Learning loops - how field data feeds back into programme adaptation</li>
          <li>Reporting wireframes - what leadership, funders, and field teams each see</li>
          <li>MEL Framework document - ready to hand to funders as credible evidence</li>
        </ul>
      </div>

      <span class="card-label">Data Systems & Dashboards</span>
      <h2>The infrastructure to turn data into decisions.</h2>
      <p class="card-tagline">That your team can actually maintain.</p>
      <p class="card-body">Data exists but it's not reaching decision-makers. We audit your systems, design what you need - scaled to your team and budget - and either build it directly (Excel, Power BI, R, Python) or produce a technical requirements brief that lets you commission the right partner with confidence. We leave behind something your team can maintain without us.</p>
      <div class="card-deliverables">
        <h3>What you receive</h3>

          <li>Technical requirements brief - if a build is needed, a procurement-ready specification</li>
          <li>Handover documentation - so internal staff can maintain what's been built</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

      <p class="card-tagline">Without overstating what the evidence bears.</p>

This isn't data visualisation for its own sake - it's strategic communication backed by rigorous analysis.</p>
      <div class="card-deliverables">
        <h3>What you receive</h3>

          <li>Data visualisation - dashboards and reports that communicate rather than just display</li>
        </ul>
